Our Story

A Passion for Egypt, Born in 2000

◆

Tour Egypte was founded in 2000 by Mahmoud El Sayed, a Cairo-based entrepreneur with a deep passion for his country and for sharing authentic Egypt with European travellers.

Over 26 years, Mahmoud and his team have guided more than 10,000 travellers across Egypt — from Cairo to Abu Simbel, from the banks of the Nile to the shores of the Red Sea. Every journey is tailor-made, entirely private, never shared with a group.

What sets Tour Egypte apart is a simple philosophy: you deserve to discover Egypt at your own pace, with a guide dedicated exclusively to you, in a private air-conditioned vehicle, with no compromise on quality. An official operator licensed by the Egyptian Ministry of Tourism under Licence No. 1200, Tour Egypte operates to the highest standards of professionalism, punctuality, and personalised service.

Why Egypt?

A Land Like No Other

◆

Egypt is not merely a destination — it is an encounter with the very foundations of human civilisation. Nowhere else on Earth can you stand before monuments built four and a half millennia ago, sail a river that has nourished empires for eternity, and feel the weight of history in every stone beneath your feet.

From the otherworldly silence of the Valley of the Kings in Luxor, to the colossal gaze of Abu Simbel rising at dawn, to the crystalline turquoise of the Red Sea — Egypt offers a breadth of wonder that few places can match. Cairo's medieval Islamic quarter buzzes with life; Aswan's granite islands are draped in serenity; the Nile at sunset turns gold, rose, and crimson in a display of light that has captivated painters and poets for centuries.

We believe Egypt deserves to be experienced slowly, privately, and with deep understanding. That is what Tour Egypte has been offering since 2000 — and why our travellers return, again and again, to share this extraordinary land with those they love.
